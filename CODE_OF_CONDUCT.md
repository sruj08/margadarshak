# Code of Conduct
We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:
- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Welcome newcomers.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

**Field work** with study participants, RTO staff and driving schools follows the ethics rules in `docs/08-project/FIELD_STUDY.md`.

**Reporting:** raise concerns with the maintainers listed in `docs/08-project/TEAM.md`. Reports are handled confidentially.
